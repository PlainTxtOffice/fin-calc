## About

Fin Calc helps you check figures, review how you reached a total, and reuse
earlier results without retyping them. It has apps for Windows, Android, iPhone,
and iPad. Your calculation history and settings stay on each device between
sessions; they do not sync across devices.

## Features

- Calculate with decimals, parentheses, powers, and base-10 logarithms. Decimal
  arithmetic gives `0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3`.
- Review saved calculations and reuse their full-precision results in the
  graphical apps.
- Export the calculation tape as CSV from the Windows graphical app and the
  Android and iOS apps.
- Choose light or dark appearance and full precision or 0–6 displayed decimal
  places. Display rounding leaves saved results unchanged.
- Use a graphical window, a terminal interface, or a command-line calculator on
  Windows.
- **Paid subscription · Android · Available on Google Play:** estimate
  enterprise value, equity value, and value per share with the premium DCF
  calculator. Enter cash-flow assumptions, a high-growth period, an optional
  fade to terminal growth, and a discount rate; review the yearly forecast and
  copy the valuation report.
